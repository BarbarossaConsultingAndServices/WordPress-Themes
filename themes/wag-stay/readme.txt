Cheerful pet boarding & daycare block theme. Services with prices, booking, gallery, journal — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Wag Stay ships Home, Services & Prices (boarding/daycare/grooming/training/vet), About, Happy Guests Gallery, Booking, FAQ, Journal posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Terracotta default, Midnight Walk, Forest Trail, Berry Collar, Ocean Pup, Honey Mono). Photos: bundled Pexels images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload wag-stay.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo pet-care site (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
