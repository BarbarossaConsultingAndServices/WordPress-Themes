Romantic wedding planner & venue block theme. Collections with prices, real weddings, vendors, booking, gallery, journal — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Evermore Weddings ships Home, Collections & packages (elopement/signature/estate), Real weddings stories, Trusted vendors, About, Moments gallery, Check your date booking, FAQ, Journal posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Rosewood default, Midnight Waltz, Pure Ivory, Sage Vows, Champagne Toast, Dusty Blue). Photos: bundled Pexels + Unsplash images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload evermore-weddings.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo wedding site (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
