Elegant boutique-hotel block theme. Rooms with rates, booking, experiences, gallery, journal — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Juniper House ships Home, Rooms & Rates (4 room types with prices), About (story + hosts), Experiences (water, trails, slow mornings), Gallery, Booking (form placeholder, policies), FAQ, Journal posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Pine default, Midnight Pine, Santorini Light, Burgundy Estate, Desert Dune, Nordic Mono). Photos: bundled Pexels images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload juniper-house.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo hotel (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
