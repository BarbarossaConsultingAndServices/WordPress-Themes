Calm medical-clinic block theme with services/doctors/pricing/booking — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Vitalia Clinic ships Home, Treatments & services, Our doctors, Prices & insurance, About, Clinic tour Gallery, Book a visit, FAQ, Health journal posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Teal default, Midnight Shift, Clinical White, Mint Ward, Deep Ocean, Warm Care). Photos: bundled Pexels + Unsplash images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload vitalia-clinic.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo clinic site (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
