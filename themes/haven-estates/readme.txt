Elegant real estate block theme with listings, sell, agents — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Haven Estates ships Home, Listings, Sell with us, Our agents, About, Book a viewing, FAQ, Interiors gallery, Journal posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Navy/Brass default, Midnight Keys, Ivory Minimal, Harbor Mist, Golden Hour, Slate Contract). Photos: bundled Pexels + Unsplash images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload haven-estates.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo real-estate site (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
