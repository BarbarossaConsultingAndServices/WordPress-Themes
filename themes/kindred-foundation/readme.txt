Warm charity & nonprofit block theme with causes, impact, volunteer and donate pages — 100% Gutenberg, 6 palettes, auto-built demo site on activation.

== Description ==

Kindred Foundation ships Home, Our Causes (food/education/elder care/environment/medical/relief), Your Impact, Volunteer, Donate, About, Field Moments Gallery, FAQ, Stories posts + full templates + 6 palettes (Leaf default, Midnight Mission, Pure Canvas, Ocean Cause, Sunset Drive, Plum Giving). Photos: bundled Pexels + Unsplash images, free to use.

== Installation ==

Upload kindred-foundation.zip via Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > Activate. The complete demo charity site (pages, posts, menu, homepage) builds itself. Switch palettes in Editor > Styles.
